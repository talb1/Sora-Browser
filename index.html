Privacy First

Sora includes built-in protections that stop sites from tracking or identifying you — before a page even loads.

Lightning Fast

Built entirely in SwiftUI on WebKit. No Electron, no Chromium overhead. Just a lean native app that starts in under a second.

◧

Vertical Tabs

A collapsible sidebar that pins to a floating icon strip when you want it out of the way. Your layout, your rules.

🔒

Private Windows

Spin up a private session in one keystroke. No history, no cookies, no trace — gone the moment you close it.

✋

Ad & Tracker Blocking

Network-level blocking keeps ads and trackers off your pages. See the live count on your new tab page.

👁

Fingerprint Protection

Canvas, WebGL, screen geometry, and more are masked so sites can't build a unique profile of your device.
