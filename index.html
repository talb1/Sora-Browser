Features

Privacy First

Canvas fingerprinting, WebRTC leaks, timezone inference, and font enumeration — all blocked before a page can read them.

⚡

Lightning Fast

Built entirely in SwiftUI on WebKit. No Electron, no Chromium overhead. Just a lean native app that starts in under a second.

◧

Vertical Tabs

A collapsible sidebar that pins to a floating icon strip when you want it out of the way. Your layout, your rules.

🔒

Private Windows

Spin up a private session in one keystroke. No history, no cookies, no trace — gone the moment you close it.

📡

Encrypted DNS

Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 by default. Every lookup is encrypted so your ISP can't see which sites you visit.

✋

Ad Blocking

Network-level ad and tracker blocking powered by EasyList, updated weekly. See the live count on your new tab page.
