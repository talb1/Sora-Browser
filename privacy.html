Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours.

Sora is a local app. No servers, no accounts, no analytics. There's nothing to collect and nowhere to send it.

🚫

No data collected

Sora never collects, stores, or transmits any personal information about you or your browsing.

📡

No servers

Built-in protections

Data Collection

What we collect

Nothing. Sora does not collect any data about you, your browsing, or your device. There are no analytics, no crash reporters, no telemetry, and no usage tracking of any kind.

Local Storage

What stays on your Mac

Sora stores the following data locally on your device, in your user directory. None of it ever leaves your Mac.

  • Browsing history — stored locally, clearable at any time from Settings → History
  • Saved passwords — stored in your macOS Keychain, never in plaintext
  • Site permissions — per-domain camera, microphone, and JavaScript preferences saved to a local JSON file
  • Settings & preferences — theme, search engine, and other preferences stored via UserDefaults
  • Favicons — cached locally to avoid re-fetching on every visit

Network Requests

What Sora connects to

Sora makes network requests on your behalf when you browse. The only requests Sora initiates automatically are favicon fetching and periodic blocklist refreshes. Everything else is a direct result of your navigation.

  • Search suggestions — sent to your chosen search engine as you type in the address bar
  • Favicons — fetched to display site icons in your tab list
  • Blocklist updates — fetched periodically to keep ad and tracker blocking current
  • Update checks — Sora periodically checks for new versions so you stay up to date

Third Parties

Third-party websites

When you visit a website in Sora, that website's own privacy policy applies. Sora's built-in ad and tracker blocking reduces the number of third parties that can observe your activity, but we have no control over the sites you choose to visit.

Browser Fingerprinting

Built-in fingerprint protection

Sora injects privacy hardening into every page before it loads. The following browser APIs are spoofed or restricted to prevent sites from building a unique fingerprint of your device:

  • Canvas fingerprinting — pixel readback is noise-seeded per session so identical hardware returns different values each time
  • WebGL vendor strings — GPU make and model are masked as a generic "Apple GPU"
  • WebRTC IP leaks — raw LAN IP candidates are filtered; only relay and server-reflexive candidates are allowed
  • Timezone inference — Intl.DateTimeFormat and Date.getTimezoneOffset are clamped to UTC
  • Screen geometry — screen dimensions and device pixel ratio are normalized to common values
  • Font enumeration — document.fonts iteration is blocked; web font loading continues to work normally
  • Performance timing — performance.now() precision is reduced to 1 ms to defeat timing-based fingerprints
